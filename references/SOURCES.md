they do not validate a historical claim or establish live-provider capabilities.

conversation is distributed.

## Internal project design decisions

Earlier internal design notes informed staged creative work, scratch narration, one dominant
action per generated unit, explicit visual anchors, endpoint reachability, bridge/reset routes,
motion-first prompting, failure classification and role-based editorial review.
Those notes are not publicly published sources and are not cited as public research evidence.
The public repository describes the resulting decisions in its own words.

The project's added historical-integrity layer includes claim-level ledgers, allowed wording,
reconstruction disclosure, source-region overlays, hash-bound receipts, limited human gates
and explicit distinctions between planning, preview, acceptance and final delivery.

Institution-specific material belongs to the [optional historical example](../examples/jnu-gate/README.md).
Treat its factual assertions as example claims requiring independent sources, not defaults or
framework-endorsed evidence. MIT applies to this code and documentation; external source-asset
rights must be assessed separately.
