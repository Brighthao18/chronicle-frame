# Community code of conduct

Treat contributors and people represented in historical material with respect. Welcome questions
and different perspectives; discuss evidence, claims and implementation without personal attacks.
Disclose uncertainty and source disagreements, and make corrections without misrepresenting others.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, nonconsensual sharing of personal data and repeated
disruptive behavior are unacceptable. Do not publish private participant details, research documents,
credentials or source media without the necessary permission.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation proportionately. Report
conduct concerns through a verified private maintainer channel when one is available; if no such
channel is listed, request a private contact without exposing the affected person's details publicly.
Maintainers should explain actions where safe and allow relevant context or corrections.

This original policy applies to project issues, reviews, discussions and related community spaces.
It does not claim an adopted third-party code or an established enforcement staff.
