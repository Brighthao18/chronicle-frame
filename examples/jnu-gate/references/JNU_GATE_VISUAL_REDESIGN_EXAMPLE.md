# JNU Gate Concept — Visual Synthesis Worked Example

Use this only as a **worked director example**, not as locked historical truth or a required storyboard. Re-check all dates/captions against the project evidence ledger.

The purpose is to demonstrate how the existing gate/photo material can become cinema without animating historical people.

## The weak pattern to avoid

> full photo → slow push → next photo → slow pan → dissolve → next photo

Changing crop direction does not create a new cinematic idea.

## Example sequence design

### S01 — “穿过今天，进入档案”

**Asset set:** `GATE-NOW`, `A269`, `GATE-NJ-R`, `A093`, `A089-R/A089`, `A090-R/A090`

**Primary engine:** portal / threshold  
**Secondary:** archival paper world  
**Sound engine:** one present-day footstep becomes page/film movement

**Visual sentence:** The viewer approaches the dark opening of the present gate; instead of cutting to an old photograph, the gate opening becomes a matte into a shallow archival space where the Nanjing gate, the 1907 departure image, and the arrival image occupy different depth planes; the camera discovers them as one connected historical movement, then exits through the geometry of the old gate.

Possible shot logic:
1. Present gate: camera advances toward dark opening. A foreground pillar wipes frame.
2. Inside darkness, a thin white archival-photo edge appears; camera crosses the edge as if entering a document table in depth.
3. `GATE-NJ-R` holds the architectural center while `A089` appears as a foreground evidence card; reveal its verified banner/date area first, then allow the whole photograph to settle into frame. People remain absolutely still.
4. A page-edge movement carries screen direction; `A090` arrives not as “the next slide” but as the destination of that movement. A restrained route/date graphic connects the two source cards.
5. The camera changes depth rather than simply zooming: foreground `A089` moves out of focus, midground `A090` remains readable, background gate geometry grows to fill the frame.
6. The gate arch becomes an occlusion/match shape leading to the next historical node.

### S02 — “照片不是章节，线条才是变化”

**Asset set:** `A091-R/A091`, `A092-R/A092`, `A291`, `GATE-SH-R`, `A292`

**Primary engine:** document-to-world  
**Secondary:** geometry match

**Visual sentence:** A verified detail in the 1909 gate photograph becomes a drawn structural line; that line continues into the real campus plan, traces a route/axis, and resolves into the geometry of the Shanghai gate, turning “from school to university” into a visual construction rather than a captioned dissolve.

Possible devices:
- Use `A091/A092` as layered evidence on one virtual archival table, not separate full-screen shots.
- Protect all faces/text; motion comes from camera depth and masks.
- Let a line extracted/redrawn from a verified architectural edge continue into `A291` as a graphic overlay.
- The plan line reaches the gate location/axis and expands into `A292/GATE-SH-R` through a geometry match.
- Keep the original and restored gate briefly visible together as evidence/reconstruction layers before settling on the restored view.

### S03 — “中断不是黑场卡片，而是结构被切断”

**Asset set:** `GATE-SH-R`, `A291`, route map layer, `GATE-JY-R/A186`, `A176`, `A177`, `A184`

**Primary engine:** rupture + map-becomes-journey  
**Secondary:** paper tear / occlusion transition

**Visual sentence:** The stable Shanghai geometry loses its supporting lines; a route line continues when the architecture stops, cuts across the paper field toward Jianyang, then turns into the vertical edge of the Jianyang gate photograph; the subsequent group photographs build around that gate as evidence of continued institutional life.

Possible devices:
- Do not add flames, soldiers, explosions, or fake war footage.
- Let sound drop first (J-cut into silence), then break visual continuity by removing/interrupting the plan line.
- The map route is integrated into the spatial composition; the camera follows it, passing archival nodes rather than watching a flat line animate on a flat map.
- At Jianyang, the route line rotates/aligns with the vertical photo border of `GATE-JY-R`.
- `A176`, `A177`, `A184` can assemble progressively around the gate like a documentary contact wall. Use dates/captions as anchored evidence; people remain still.
- The sequence payoff is not “three more group photos”; it is the visual accumulation of continued activity around the same institutional threshold.

### S04 — “重建：白边变成新门”

**Asset set:** `A184`, `GATE-GZ-R`, `A126`, `A161`, `A233`, `A234`

**Primary engine:** photo-border morph / reconstruction window  
**Secondary:** contact-sheet montage

**Visual sentence:** The white edge of the final Jianyang photograph expands until it becomes the bright architectural boundary of the Guangzhou gate; instead of presenting later events one by one, opening-ceremony and meeting images form a controlled archive contact sheet whose internal rhythm accelerates toward institutional restoration.

Possible devices:
- Use the white border as a full-frame occlusion, then reveal `GATE-GZ-R` aligned to the new bright shape.
- `A126` may sit temporarily inside the reconstructed gate view as a visible source card, preserving the distinction between source evidence and reconstructed extension.
- `A161`, `A233`, `A234` enter as a montage cluster with shared alignment/date rhythm, not three identical full-screen pushes.
- Sound can shift from page/wood texture into larger interior crowd-space design without pretending it is authentic historical sync sound.

### S05 — “五扇门不是五张图，而是一条镜头路径”

**Asset set:** all approved gate images + `GATE-NOW/A269` + selected human/document anchors from prior sequences

**Primary engine:** nested thresholds / geometry match montage  
**Secondary:** rhythmic archival callbacks

**Visual sentence:** Rather than cutting five gate photos at 1.2 seconds each, the camera moves through a constructed tunnel of successive gate geometries; each threshold briefly reveals one already-established human/document detail from that era in its negative space, so the final present gate contains the memory of the earlier ones.

Possible devices:
- Use approved gate silhouettes/arches as successive depth planes.
- Keep each gate's geometry exact; do not morph one building elastically into another.
- Cut or occlude only when major structural lines align.
- Let a recurring footstep/page/door sound change timbre at each threshold.
- Final present-day gate should not simply “push in then pull out.” Decide one directional statement and complete it.

## Design lesson

The material library is a vocabulary, not a playlist. The director's task is to create **relationships, causality, spatial continuity, rhythmic contrast, and visual memory** among sources.

A source may appear:
- full frame once,
- as a crop/detail elsewhere,
- as a foreground card,
- as a matte/occlusion,
- as a geometry reference,
- as a background evidence layer,
- as a callback in the final montage.

This is acceptable as long as provenance is preserved and the edit does not misrepresent the source.

### Example production shot — not merely a visual idea

**SHOT S01-SH03 — ~4.5 s**

- **Story purpose / director intent:** make the audience feel that the present gate is physically opening into documented history, not cutting to a slideshow.
- **Reference IDs:** `ARCH001 + ARCH002 + DOC001 + STYLE001`
- **Shot size / lens feel:** wide-to-medium axial view, ~35 mm feel.
- **Camera position:** eye level, centered on the present gate opening.
- **Primary action:** the camera crosses one foreground pillar/occlusion and reveals the archival depth space beyond it.
- **Secondary motion:** subtle paper-edge parallax only; all historical people remain static.
- **Transition:** the present pillar becomes the wipe/matte that exposes `DOC001`, while `ARCH002` is already visible deeper in frame.
- **Continuity locks:** exact gate proportions, original document crop, no invented people, no altered Chinese signage.
- **Generation risk:** AI may elastically morph the gate or animate faces.
- **Preferred route:** composite approved still layers / depth planes first, then animate camera/parallax.
- **Safer fallback:** build the entire shot in 2.5D/post using cut masks and a virtual camera; no generative person motion.

This is stronger than asking a model to “make the old photograph cinematic.” The desired camera path and evidence relationship are explicit, and the fallback preserves the story function if generative motion fails.

### Example Animatic cue

For S01, do not choose final shot durations until a scratch narration read and temp sound pass exists.

Suggested animatic test:
1. present footstep begins **before** the gate fills frame;
2. scratch VO states the opening question;
3. the footstep tail becomes a page/paper sound as the camera crosses the pillar;
4. hold the first readable archival evidence long enough for the audience to recognize it;
5. shorten any decorative depth movement that delays the first factual discovery;
6. do not enter S02 until the Nanjing gate geometry has visually become the exit target.

If the VO forces the archival evidence to flash by too quickly, cut narration before sacrificing evidence readability.


## v1.4 anchor-frame upgrade

Before using any detailed shot design from this example, run the project-specific sequence in `JNU_GATE_PROJECT_MODE.md`:

1. build 3 opening propositions;
2. select an opening based on silent comprehension + evidence safety;
3. design the final frame as a visible transformation of that opening;
4. build the gate asset/motif relationship graph;
5. create the attention/state-change map;
6. only then write the new Sequence Design and shot board.

For the current project, the strongest default hypothesis is **not** “show the present gate, then reveal old gates.” It is “the present threshold visibly contains/aligns with historical evidence, creating an impossible-but-clearly-metaphorical depth that the film gradually explains.” The final shot should reverse or resolve that visual proposition rather than simply return to another slow push on the present gate.

For I2V versions of these transitions, the start frame carries appearance/geometry; the prompt should mostly specify layer motion, camera path, timing, and exact end state. Historical people in archival photographs remain frozen unless the user deliberately chooses a disclosed stylized reenactment.
