## Project-specific note for 《门外，是四海》

Use chaining especially for:
- moving through one gate layer into another;
- route/map line continuation;
- archive-border reveals;
- present-gate ↔ historical-gate match continuities;
- evidence-wall accumulation in Jianyang.

Do **not** use chaining to fake historical people performing complex actions. For archive-safe work, prefer motion in camera, graphics, paper layers, light, focus, and compositing.



## Extracted from references/providers/FLOW_SHOT_ENDPOINT_SYSTEM.md

## 10. 《门外，是四海》 defaults

### Present-day gate
Use a source-locked or approved edited gate frame. Preserve Chinese signage and gate geometry.

### Present → historical gate
Default to axis match, threshold occlusion, paper-edge reset, or a deterministic junction frame. Do not use a free cross-era morph.

### Archive evidence walls
Build layout as approved still assets first. Flow may animate camera/paper depth only.

### Route/map
Exact labels and route geometry remain deterministic/post. Flow is optional for background depth, not cartographic truth.

### Historical people
People inside source photographs remain still. Do not use Flow endpoint conditioning to manufacture action that never existed in the source.



## Extracted from references/providers/FLOW_CINEMATIC_PROMPTING.md

## 10. 《门外，是四海》 prompt signatures

### A. Present-day threshold

Prompt should feel photographic and spatial, not promotional:
- specific time/light from approved frame;
- restrained observational move;
- campus ambience;
- threshold as focal geometry;
- no generic `epic university`, `majestic`, `inspiring` language.

### B. Archive-space move

Prompt should specify physical layer logic:
- paper planes at different depths;
- one foreground layer passes close to lens;
- historical image remains a fixed evidence surface;
- camera movement creates parallax;
- no animation of people within photographs.

### C. Gate-to-gate continuity

Do not request elastic architectural morphing.
Use:
- occlusion;
- matching axis;
- matching arch silhouette;
- controlled reset / second segment;
- or explicit Frames path only when reachability has passed.

### D. Route / migration

The precise map and labels are deterministic/post layers.
Flow may animate:
- camera hovering over a prepared map composition;
- paper texture/light;
- a controlled route-line reveal if the route is already graphically correct.

### E. Jianyang evidence accumulation

The core cinematic idea is **evidence becoming density**.
Prefer deterministic composition plus a controlled camera move, not AI-generated people or free collage rearrangement.
